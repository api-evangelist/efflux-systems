# Efflux (Efflux Systems, Inc.)

> Efflux is a Baltimore, Maryland cybersecurity company (founded 2014) operating a near real-time asset intelligence and attack-surface platform. It delivers fresh intelligence about the assets of organizations, networks, and services — as one-time Asset Maps, scheduled monitoring, or on-demand custom scanning — with a transparency-first model that records and exposes every scan, discovered asset, and attribution rather than opaque risk scores. Data is available via export, download, share, and a programmatic API. Currently in early access with a public waitlist.

## Platform
- Asset Map: One-time discovery of an organization's assets across networks and services.
- Scheduled monitoring: Recurring scans on a timeline the customer sets.
- On-demand custom scanning: Ad hoc scans when needed.
- Full-fidelity evidence: Raw information and formatted insights side by side — what was discovered, how, and why it matters.
